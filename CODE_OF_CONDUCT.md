# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this to be a welcoming place to learn, especially for people who
are completely new to Spring Boot or to programming in general. We pledge
to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or identity.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps create a positive environment:

- Being patient and kind with beginners — everyone was new once.
- Giving constructive feedback on content, not on people.
- Gracefully accepting feedback on your own contributions.
- Focusing on what's best for learners using this tutorial.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Insulting or demeaning comments, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Dismissive responses to beginner questions ("just Google it" is not
  in the spirit of this project).
- Any other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported by opening an issue
or contacting the maintainers directly. All complaints will be reviewed
and investigated fairly. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject
comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that don't
align with this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull
requests, discussions) and when an individual is representing the
project in public spaces.
